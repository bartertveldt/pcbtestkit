Weak points

#SeverityFindingWhy it matters / what to doParts / nets
F001major2 copper item(s) closer than 0.30 mm to the board edge
MFG-EDGE-CLEARANCE · manufacturing		Routing/scoring exposes or burrs copper that sits this close to the edge (shorts to enclosure, delamination).
Pull copper back to >= 0.38 mm from Edge.Cuts or ask the fab for edge plating.		J1
Net-(J1-MP1)
F002major12 hole pair(s) closer than 0.25 mm
MFG-HOLE-SPACING · manufacturing		Thin webs between drills crack or merge during drilling.
Increase spacing between the holes.
F003majorBoard is finer than Eurocircuits supports (pattern)
MFG-TECH-CLASS · manufacturing		No level of Eurocircuits pattern + drill classes accepts these features: via annular ring (outer) 0.025 at pattern 9 (limit 0.1); via annular ring (inner) 0.025 at pattern 9 (limit 0.1); via hole to inner-layer copper 0.155 at pattern 9 (limit 0.2); via hole (finished) 0.3 at drill B (limit 0.35).
Widen the listed features, or choose a finer process or another class system.
F004major2 plated hole pad(s) with annular ring < 0.15 mm
MFG-THT-ANNULAR · manufacturing		Thin rings lift during hand or wave soldering and give weak solder fillets.
Enlarge the pads; a ring of >= 0.25 mm is a good target for hand-soldered parts.		SW501
F005major444 via(s) with annular ring below 0.100 mm
MFG-VIA · manufacturing		Drill wander can break out of a thin annular ring, giving intermittent or open connections.
Increase via pad diameter (size - drill >= 0.25 mm).
F006majorNo fiducials on the top side (54 SMD parts)
ASM-FIDUCIALS · assembly		Pick-and-place machines need >= 2 global fiducials per populated side for accurate placement.
Add 2-3 fiducials in opposite corners of the top side, or confirm that the panel rails carry them.
F007majorNo fiducials on the bottom side (14 SMD parts)
ASM-FIDUCIALS · assembly		Pick-and-place machines need >= 2 global fiducials per populated side for accurate placement.
Add 2-3 fiducials in opposite corners of the bottom side, or confirm that the panel rails carry them.
F008major9 small part(s) with unbalanced pad heating
ASM-TOMBSTONE · assembly		One pad heats much slower than the other (solid plane, wide track or via); surface tension then lifts the part on end during reflow (tombstoning/open joint).
Use thermal reliefs on the plane connection and equal-width tracks on both pads.		C101, C102, C401, C501, C502, C503, C707, C712, R107
F009major1 input/power pin(s) left unconnected
ELEC-FLOATING · electrical		Floating CMOS inputs oscillate, draw extra current and cause random behaviour; unconnected power pins leave parts of the IC unpowered.
Tie unused inputs to a defined level (per datasheet) and connect all power pins.		Module301
F010major4 power net(s) cannot be probed
TEST-POWER-ACCESS · testability		Rails without probe access cannot be verified on the production tester.
Add a >= 0.9 mm test pad on the top side for each listed rail.
+1V8, +BATT, /CM5/PWR_BUT, HDMI_+5V
F011major1 board footprint(s) not in the schematic
DOC-SCH-SYNC · documentation		Extra footprints are not in the BOM/netlist (unless intended as board-only).
Mark them 'Not in schematic' (board only) or add symbols.		REF**
F012minor35 acute track junction(s) (< 80 deg)
MFG-ACID-TRAP · manufacturing		Acute copper angles trap etchant and can over-etch, creating thin necks.
Re-route the junctions with 45/90 degree entries.
F013minor1 copper item(s) close to the board edge
MFG-EDGE-CLEARANCE · manufacturing		Between 0.30 and 0.38 mm from the edge.
Add margin where cheap to do so.
F014minor8 plated hole pad(s) with annular ring close to the minimum
MFG-THT-ANNULAR · manufacturing		Rings within 25% of the fab limit.
Enlarge pads where possible.		J101, USB701
F015minor1379 track segment(s) close to the 0.127 mm fab minimum
MFG-TRACK-WIDTH · manufacturing		Narrowest track: 0.130 mm. Over-etching on thin tracks causes opens and lower yield.
Widen tracks to at least 0.159 mm where space allows, or choose a fab profile that supports them.
+1V8, +3V3_PI, /CC1, /CC2, /CM5/DPHY0_P.C_N, /CM5/DPHY0_P.C_P, /CM5/DPHY0_P.D0_N, /CM5/DPHY0_P.D0_P, /CM5/DPHY0_P.D1_N, /CM5/DPHY0_P.D1_P
F016minor4 component(s) within 0.5 mm of (or over) the edge
ASM-COMPONENT-EDGE · assembly		Parts at the edge collide with panel rails, conveyor clamps and depaneling tools.
Keep courtyards >= 3.0 mm inside the outline or add breakaway rails.		D605, D606, D702, SW501
F017minor11 fine-pitch (<= 0.5 mm) part(s)
ASM-FINE-PITCH · assembly		Fine pitch raises the risk of solder bridges and insufficient joints.
Use a <= 0.12 mm stencil with reduced apertures, solder-mask dams between pads and AOI after reflow.		D401, D402, D403, J701, J802, Module301, Module302, U1, U401, U402, U702
F018minorAssembly process: SMT top (54 parts) + SMT bottom (14 parts) + THT (5 parts: wave, selective or hand soldering)
ASM-PROCESS · assembly		Each extra process step adds cost and handling (handling = flex & ESD exposure). Bottom SMD + THT usually needs selective soldering or a wave pallet.
The generated solder jig holds the THT parts for hand soldering.		J1, J501, TP104, TP105, TP106
F019minor2 flex-sensitive part(s) within 3 mm of mounting holes
REL-MOUNTING · reliability		Screw torque bends the board locally and cracks MLCCs / BGA joints.
Keep MLCCs and area-array packages away from screws or use standoffs with washers.		C101, C709
F020minor5 connector(s) without through-hole anchors
REL-SMD-CONNECTOR · reliability		Mating forces and cable strain go straight into SMD solder joints; pads rip off after repeated use.
Use connector variants with through-hole shell tabs/pegs, or add an enclosure strain relief.		CN1, J502, J503, J601, J802
F021minor1 exposed pad(s) without thermal vias
REL-THERMAL-PAD · reliability		Without vias the exposed pad cannot spread heat; the part runs hot and ages faster.
Add an array of 0.3 mm vias (tented or filled on the bottom to limit solder voids).		U702
F022minor1 IC power net(s) lack a nearby decoupling capacitor
ELEC-DECOUPLING · electrical		Distant decoupling adds loop inductance: supply noise, brown-out resets and EMC failures that show up unit-to-unit and over temperature.
Place a 100 nF capacitor within 5 mm of every power pin (plus bulk capacitance per rail).		U602
+5V
F023minor2 external connector(s) with unprotected signal lines
ELEC-ESD · electrical		ESD at user-accessible connectors is a top cause of field returns (latent damage, not caught by test).
Add TVS/ESD diodes close to the connector (e.g. USBLC6 for USB, low-capacitance arrays for data lines).		J101, USB701
/CC1, /CC2, /USB/CC1, /USB/CC2
F024minor8 power net(s) contain tracks narrower than 0.30 mm
REL-POWER-TRACKS · electrical		Thin power tracks drop voltage and heat up; capacity shown is IPC-2221 at 10 C rise (35 um Cu). Set nets.<name>.max_current_a in the config to turn this into a hard check.
Widen power tracks where space allows.
+1V8, +3V3_PI, +5V, +BATT, /CM5/PWR_BUT, GND, HDMI_+5V, M2_3V3
F025minor17 capacitor(s) on known rails have no voltage rating
DOC-CAP-RATING · documentation		Without a rating the derating cannot be verified and purchasing may substitute a low-voltage part.
Add a 'Voltage' field (e.g. 16V) to the capacitor symbols.		C101, C102, C501, C502, C503, C601, C701, C702, C703, C704, C705, C706, C708, C709, C710, C711, C712
F026minor43 of 70 part(s) have no part number
DOC-MPN · documentation		Unspecified parts get substituted, which changes ratings, footprints and long-term reliability.
Add MPN fields for the remaining parts.		C101, C102, C401, C501, C502, C503, C601, C702, C706, C707, C712, D101, D502, D605, D606, D701, D702, J501, J502, J503 ...
F027infoDiodes on the top side use 4 different orientations
ASM-POLARITY · assembly		Mixed orientations of polarized parts increase placement and visual-inspection errors.
Align polarized parts to one or two orientations and mark polarity clearly on silkscreen.		D101, D401, D402, D502, D605, D606, D701, D702
F028info11 probe-side part(s) taller than the fixture clearance
TEST-FIXTURE-CLEARANCE · testability		The fixture board gets cut-outs for these parts and no probes are placed under them.
Check the cut-outs in the generated fixture board.		H101, H102, H103, H104, H105, J1, J501, M702, TP104, TP105, TP106
F029info77 signal net(s) not probed
TEST-SIGNAL-ACCESS · testability		These nets are not covered by the bed-of-nails test.
Add test pads, raise testplan.max_channels, or accept the gap.
/CC1, /CC2, /CM5/BL_GPIO24, /CM5/CAM_GPIO0, /CM5/CAM_GPIO1, /CM5/CS_GPIO18, /CM5/DC_GPI25, /CM5/DPHY0_P.C_N, /CM5/DPHY0_P.C_P, /CM5/DPHY0_P.D0_N
F030info2 part(s) marked DNP
DOC-DNP · documentation		These must be absent on a good unit; they are listed in the visual inspection step.
M701, R103

Fixture board

24 pogo pin(s), 3 site(s), outline rect. Routed by PCB TestKit (50 connections, 26 vias, KiCad's default rules): open it in KiCad, run DRC and order it.
Batch of 3 boards: 3 fixture sites (3 x 1), each with its own header, power terminal; the runner tests them one after the other.
