#SeverityFindingWhy it matters / what to doParts / nets
F001critical26 resistor(s) over their derated power
REL-RES-POWER · reliability		Computed from the rail voltages across the resistor (P = V^2/R).
Use a larger package/higher rating, or a higher resistance.		C9, C10, C11, C15, C17, C18, C19, C20, C21, C22, C24, C25, C26, C27, C28, C29, C30, C31, C38, C67 ...
F002majorNo fiducials on the top side (40 SMD parts)
ASM-FIDUCIALS · assembly		Pick-and-place machines need >= 2 global fiducials per populated side for accurate placement.
Add 2-3 fiducials in opposite corners of the top side, or confirm that the panel rails carry them.
F003majorNo fiducials on the bottom side (103 SMD parts)
ASM-FIDUCIALS · assembly		Pick-and-place machines need >= 2 global fiducials per populated side for accurate placement.
Add 2-3 fiducials in opposite corners of the bottom side, or confirm that the panel rails carry them.
F004major13 via(s) inside SMD pads
ASM-VIA-IN-PAD · assembly		Open vias in pads wick solder away from the joint (starved joints, voids) unless they are filled and capped.
Move vias next to the pad (dog-bone) or order filled & capped vias (IPC-4761 type VII).		C13, C39, C42, C51, C52, C70, L5, R15, R23, R36, R37, R46, U6
F005major7 MLCC(s) within 3.0 mm of the board edge
ASM-MLCC-EDGE · reliability		Board flexing during depaneling, ICT, connector insertion or screwing cracks MLCCs; cracks short the rail later in the field. Perpendicular orientation (terminals at different distances from the edge) is worst.
Move MLCCs >= 3.0 mm from edges and V-score lines, orient them parallel to the edge, or use soft-termination / flexible-termination parts.		C1, C8, C35, C39, C40, C41, C63
F006major18 IC power net(s) lack a nearby decoupling capacitor
ELEC-DECOUPLING · electrical		Distant decoupling adds loop inductance: supply noise, brown-out resets and EMC failures that show up unit-to-unit and over temperature.
Place a 100 nF capacitor within 5 mm of every power pin (plus bulk capacitance per rail).		U1, U2, U3, U4, U5, U6, U12, U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U18, U19, U21, U22, U23, U24
+5V
F007majorNo supply net identified for powered tests
TEST-SUPPLY · testability		no power net reaches a connector; set supply.net
Set supply.net (and supply.voltage) in pcbtestkit.json.
F008minor18 acute track junction(s) (< 80 deg)
MFG-ACID-TRAP · manufacturing		Acute copper angles trap etchant and can over-etch, creating thin necks.
Re-route the junctions with 45/90 degree entries.
F009minor1 copper item(s) close to the board edge
MFG-EDGE-CLEARANCE · manufacturing		Between 0.30 and 0.38 mm from the edge.
Add margin where cheap to do so.
F010minor3 large/heavy SMD part(s) on the bottom side
ASM-BOTTOM-HEAVY · assembly		In double-sided reflow, heavy bottom parts can drop or shift during the second pass.
Move them to the top side or specify adhesive dots for the bottom side.		U3, U4, U5
F011minor5 component(s) within 0.5 mm of (or over) the edge
ASM-COMPONENT-EDGE · assembly		Parts at the edge collide with panel rails, conveyor clamps and depaneling tools.
Keep courtyards >= 3.0 mm inside the outline or add breakaway rails.		BUS1, C8, R20, R35, U6
F012minor1 fine-pitch (<= 0.5 mm) part(s)
ASM-FINE-PITCH · assembly		Fine pitch raises the risk of solder bridges and insufficient joints.
Use a <= 0.12 mm stencil with reduced apertures, solder-mask dams between pads and AOI after reflow.		U22
F013minorAssembly process: SMT top (40 parts) + SMT bottom (103 parts) + THT (45 parts: wave, selective or hand soldering)
ASM-PROCESS · assembly		Each extra process step adds cost and handling (handling = flex & ESD exposure). Bottom SMD + THT usually needs selective soldering or a wave pallet.
The generated solder jig holds the THT parts for hand soldering.		C49, C50, CV1, D1, D2, D3, D4, D6, J4, L6, P1, P2, P3, P4, P5, P8, P9, P10, P11, P12 ...
F014minorNo mounting holes on a 312 x 107 mm board
REL-MOUNTING · reliability		Unsupported boards flex and vibrate; the fixture also needs tooling holes for alignment.
Add >= 3 mounting holes (they double as test-fixture guide-pin holes).
F015minor1 external connector(s) with unprotected signal lines
ELEC-ESD · electrical		ESD at user-accessible connectors is a top cause of field returns (latent damage, not caught by test).
Add TVS/ESD diodes close to the connector (e.g. USBLC6 for USB, low-capacitance arrays for data lines).		J4
/BLUE_OUT, /C_OUT, /GREEN_OUT, /RED_OUT, /Y_OUT
F016minor5 power net(s) contain tracks narrower than 0.30 mm
REL-POWER-TRACKS · electrical		Thin power tracks drop voltage and heat up; capacity shown is IPC-2221 at 10 C rise (35 um Cu). Set nets.<name>.max_current_a in the config to turn this into a hard check.
Widen power tracks where space allows.
+3.3V, +5F, +5V, /ESVIDEO-RVB/VAA, GND
F017minorNo dedicated test points
TEST-TESTPOINTS · testability		The fixture has to probe component pads and THT leads; probes on solder joints wear tips and can hide cracked joints.
Add >= 1 mm test pads on the probe side for every rail and key signal (see the test plan coverage).
F018minor28 capacitor(s) on known rails have no voltage rating
DOC-CAP-RATING · documentation		Without a rating the derating cannot be verified and purchasing may substitute a low-voltage part.
Add a 'Voltage' field (e.g. 16V) to the capacitor symbols.		C9, C10, C11, C15, C17, C18, C19, C20, C21, C22, C24, C25, C26, C27, C28, C29, C30, C31, C38, C56 ...
F019infoBoard needs Eurocircuits class 4B (PCB proto, STANDARD pool, Defined impedance, RF pool, Semi-flex pool, BINDI pool)
MFG-TECH-CLASS · manufacturing		Driven by: via annular ring (outer) 0.15 at pattern 3 (limit 0.2); via annular ring (inner) 0.15 at pattern 3 (limit 0.2); outer track width 0.2 at pattern 3 (limit 0.25); outer copper gap 0.1995 at pattern 3 (limit 0.25); inner track width 0.2 at pattern 3 (limit 0.25); inner copper gap 0.2 at pattern 3 (limit 0.25).
Set the class you will order (tech.class) to check the board against its limits.
F020info278 signal net(s) not probed
TEST-SIGNAL-ACCESS · testability		These nets are not covered by the bed-of-nails test.
Add test pads, raise testplan.max_channels, or accept the gap.
/ACCES_RAM-, /ACQ_ON, /ADR2, /ADR3, /ADR4, /ADR5, /ADR6, /BE-0, /BE-1, /BE-2
F021infoNo manufacturer part numbers in the schematic
DOC-MPN · documentation		The BOM relies on generic values; the assembler chooses parts.
Add an MPN field (or LCSC/Digikey numbers) to control what gets assembled.
