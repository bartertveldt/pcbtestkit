Swapping to another board type

1
Switch the station's supplies off and unscrew the fixture.
2
Plug the next board type's fixture onto connector J1 and screw it down in the same holes.
3
Run that board's runner (its folder above): its testplan.json knows which station pins its boards use.
4
Every board has its own nest and toggle clamp on the fixture: slide a board into each nest up to the stop (above the pogo pins), close its clamp, and the runner tests the boards one by one; open a clamp to take that board off.
