F001majorNo fiducials on the bottom side (1 SMD parts)
ASM-FIDUCIALS · assembly		Pick-and-place machines need >= 2 global fiducials per populated side for accurate placement.
Add 2-3 fiducials in opposite corners of the bottom side, or confirm that the panel rails carry them.
F002major1 electrolytic capacitor(s) within 10 mm of a heat source
REL-ELCAP-HEAT · reliability		Electrolytic life roughly halves for every 10 C rise (Arrhenius); electrolyte dries out and ESR rises, a classic end-of-life failure of power supplies.
Move the capacitors away from regulators/MOSFETs/inductors, use 105 C long-life series or polymer capacitors.		C1, U3, U4
F003major5 IC power net(s) lack a nearby decoupling capacitor
ELEC-DECOUPLING · electrical		Distant decoupling adds loop inductance: supply noise, brown-out resets and EMC failures that show up unit-to-unit and over temperature.
Place a 100 nF capacitor within 5 mm of every power pin (plus bulk capacitance per rail).		U1, U2, U4, U5, U6
/pic_sockets/VCC_PIC, VCC
F004minorAssembly process: SMT bottom (1 parts) + THT (55 parts: wave, selective or hand soldering)
ASM-PROCESS · assembly		Each extra process step adds cost and handling (handling = flex & ESD exposure). Bottom SMD + THT usually needs selective soldering or a wave pallet.
The generated solder jig holds the THT parts for hand soldering.		C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6, C7, C9, D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, D6, D7, D8, D9, D10, D11, D12 ...
F005minor1 connector(s) without through-hole anchors
REL-SMD-CONNECTOR · reliability		Mating forces and cable strain go straight into SMD solder joints; pads rip off after repeated use.
Use connector variants with through-hole shell tabs/pegs, or add an enclosure strain relief.		JP1
F006minor2 tall/heavy part(s) may need staking
REL-TALL-PARTS · reliability		Under vibration and shock, tall parts lever on their solder joints and leads fatigue.
Glue/stake them to the board (RTV or hot melt), add clamps, or use lower-profile parts.		C1, C2
F007minor2 external connector(s) with unprotected signal lines
ELEC-ESD · electrical		ESD at user-accessible connectors is a top cause of field returns (latent damage, not caught by test).
Add TVS/ESD diodes close to the connector (e.g. USBLC6 for USB, low-capacitance arrays for data lines).		J1, P1
/PC-CLOCK-OUT, /PC-DATA-IN, /PC-DATA-OUT, /VPP_ON, Net-(D1-A)
F008minorNo dedicated test points
TEST-TESTPOINTS · testability		The fixture has to probe component pads and THT leads; probes on solder joints wear tips and can hide cracked joints.
Add >= 1 mm test pads on the probe side for every rail and key signal (see the test plan coverage).
F009minorNo revision in the board title block
DOC-REVISION · documentation		Test results, fixtures and field returns cannot be traced to a board revision.
Set the revision in Board Setup > Title block and print it on silkscreen (${REVISION}).
