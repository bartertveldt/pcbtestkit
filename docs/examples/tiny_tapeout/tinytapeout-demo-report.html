Move vias next to the pad (dog-bone) or order filled & capped vias (IPC-4761 type VII).		C27, J4, J7, J11
F006major6 MLCC(s) within 3.0 mm of the board edge
ASM-MLCC-EDGE · reliability		Board flexing during depaneling, ICT, connector insertion or screwing cracks MLCCs; cracks short the rail later in the field. Perpendicular orientation (terminals at different distances from the edge) is worst.
Move MLCCs >= 3.0 mm from edges and V-score lines, orient them parallel to the edge, or use soft-termination / flexible-termination parts.		C3, C7, C10, C11, C12, C13
F007major1 power net(s) cannot be probed

Fixture board

52 pogo pin(s), 4 site(s), outline rect. Routed by PCB TestKit (194 connections, 170 vias, KiCad's default rules): open it in KiCad, run DRC and order it.
Batch of 4 boards: 4 fixture sites (2 x 2) around one connector (2 x 28 pins) in the middle, with every board's supply, channels and programming lines on it.
Programming header J7 (swd, pins carry SWCLK, SWDIO): probed on every pin; fixture connector - (same); no probe on pin(s) 1 (SWDIO), 2 (SWCLK).
